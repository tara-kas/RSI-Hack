# Stating the answer

Answer the question exactly as you otherwise would. The notes below are only about how the
final answer is read; they are not a method and not a substitute for solving the problem.

A model judge extracts **one** answer from your reply and compares it to a reference.

- End with the final answer on its own line, with nothing after it.
- Give one answer only. Two candidates, or hedging such as "approximately" or "either X or
  Y", reads as ambiguous and is marked wrong.
- **Keep exact things exact.** A fraction stays a fraction, a symbolic expression stays
  symbolic, a root or logarithm stays unevaluated, unless a decimal is requested. Evaluating
  an exact result to a rounded decimal can turn a correct answer into a wrong one.
- **Match the form asked for** — stated units, stated precision, and the question's own
  notation.
- **Answer-choice questions** — give the option that matches your conclusion, and nothing
  else.
- **Answer every part** of a multi-part question, in the order asked.
- Never leave it blank: a wrong answer and no answer score the same.
